## In just a sentence or two, summarize your project. (E.g., "A website that lets you buy and sell stocks.")

a game made in Godot that follows a similar structure to Getting Over It. A legless fireman traverses up a mountain, propelling himself with a fire extinguisher, to fight a fire at the top.

## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

The game will be a 2d physics-based platforming game developed in Godot, using its physics system and scripting capabilities. The extinguisher, or main means of movement, will apply force in the opposite direction of the spray. The level will be vertically designed. Visuals and sound effects (especially) will be homemade as much as I reasonably can. It will be a standalone desktop game, ideally including features like an end scene and basic controls like volume/brightness.
